# brae 0.4.1

`interFoam` — two-phase volume-of-fluid flow on the GPU.

A case whose `controlDict` says `application interFoam` now runs with the one command, `brae`.

- **All 42 `interFoam` tutorials of OpenFOAM v2412 run**, each to its own end time: dam breaks, wave makers and
  wave tanks, sloshing tanks under 3- and 6-DoF motion, the DTC hull fixed and moving, AMI and ACMI mixers,
  porous baffles, adaptive mesh refinement, capillary rise with a contact angle.
- **Laminar, RAS (k-epsilon, k-omega SST) and LES (kEqn).** MULES and CMULES, alpha sub-cycling,
  `Euler` / `CrankNicolson` / `localEuler`, adjustable time step, restart, ASCII and binary output.
- **Held to OpenFOAM, not to expectations.** The solver's steps are compared with serial OpenFOAM field by
  field on staged tutorial rows; the suite is 856 tests.
- **The pressure solve is brae's own.** Whatever a case names for `p_rgh`, brae runs an AMG-preconditioned
  conjugate gradient on the GPU and says so. Everything else follows the case.
- **Refuses rather than substitutes.** An entry the solver does not carry stops the run and names itself.
- **Speed**, per time step against OpenFOAM on all 20 cores of the same GB10: ahead on 30 of the 42 tutorials
  at their shipped size, and on all 42 once the mesh is scaled up (1.1x to 4.9x at up to 3.6 million cells).
  Meshes of a few thousand cells are still faster on the CPU.
- **Not yet:** runtime function objects (probes, gauges, sampling) and multi-GPU. A function object's
  `adjustableRunTime` write times already trim the time step as they do in OpenFOAM.

Also in this release: a periodic pair is no longer applied twice to `pimpleFoam`'s pressure hierarchy, and
`brae --help` lists every solver from the registry.

Upgrade with `git pull && cmake --build build -j --target brae`. 856 ctests on a GB10.
